# 📊 Superstore Retail Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Superstore Retail Performance Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze sales, profit, customer segments, shipping performance, and regional trends across a retail business. The dashboard provides actionable insights into business performance, helping stakeholders identify profitable regions, top-performing products, customer purchasing behavior, and operational efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profitability.
- Analyze regional and state-wise performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Understand customer segment contribution.
- Evaluate payment and shipping preferences.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends.
- Improve decision-making through data-driven insights.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Sales Performance
- California generates the highest sales and profit among all states.
- The West region contributes significantly to overall revenue.

### Product Performance
- Office Supplies lead overall category sales.
- Phones are the highest-selling sub-category.

### Customer Behavior
- Consumer segment accounts for nearly half of all sales.
- Cash on Delivery remains the most preferred payment method.

### Logistics
- Standard Class shipping dominates order fulfillment.
- Delivery and return metrics can be monitored to improve operational efficiency.
